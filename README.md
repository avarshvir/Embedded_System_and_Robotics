# 🚀 Embedded Systems & Robotics Course 🦾

Welcome to the **Embedded Systems & Robotics** repository! This comprehensive course is designed to take you from the basics of electronics to advanced robotics and IoT integration. Whether you're a beginner or looking to enhance your skills, this curriculum offers hands-on projects, industry practices, and in-depth theory to prepare you for the tech world.

## 1. Basic Components & Electronics 💡
- **Introduction to Components**: Resistors, capacitors, diodes, transistors, and voltage regulators (7805, LM317).
- **Measuring Instruments**: Multimeters, oscilloscopes, and circuit debugging.
- **Power Supply Design**: Linear/SMPS, DC power circuits, and voltage protection.

## 2. Fundamentals of Embedded Systems 📟
- **Embedded Systems Overview**: Microcontrollers, sensors, actuators, and communication modules.
- **Hardware & Development Tools**: Arduino IDE, Keil, MPLAB, and Thonny IDE.

## 3. Embedded C & MicroPython Programming 🖥️
- **Embedded C**: Memory management, interrupts, and communication protocols (UART, SPI, I2C).
- **MicroPython**: GPIO programming, IoT integration, and PWM motor control.
- **Projects**: LED sequencing, sensor interfacing, and remote monitoring.

## 4. Robotics Fundamentals 🤖
- **Robot Types**: Industrial, mobile, and humanoid robots.
- **Mechanics**: Degrees of freedom, motors (DC/stepper/servo), and power transmission.
- **Hands-On**: Build a robotic arm and use 3D modeling tools.

## 5. Microcontroller Programming for Robotics 🎛️
- **Microcontrollers**: Arduino, STM32, ESP32.
- **Projects**: Line-following robot, obstacle-avoidance bot, and humanoid prototypes.
- **Simulation**: Proteus for circuit and robot design.

## 6. Sensors & Actuators in Robotics 🔍
- **Sensors**: Ultrasonic, IR, accelerometers, and gyroscopes.
- **Actuators**: Motor control (DC/stepper/servo), pneumatics, and hydraulics.
- **Projects**: Autonomous navigation and gesture-controlled robots.

## 7. Microcontroller Architectures 🧠
- **8051**: Home automation system.
- **PIC**: Temperature-controlled fan.
- **AVR**: Data logger.
- **ARM Cortex-M**: Robotic arm control with RTOS.

## 8. Communication Protocols 📡
- **Wired**: UART, SPI, I2C.
- **Wireless**: Bluetooth, Zigbee, LoRa, and Wi-Fi (ESP32/ESP8266).
- **Projects**: Zigbee sensor networks and Bluetooth-controlled cars.

## 9. Advanced Communication & Networking 🌐
- **Protocols**: TCP/IP, MQTT, CoAP, and cellular (GSM/NB-IoT).
- **Projects**: IoT robots with real-time monitoring and remote data visualization.

## 10. IoT Integration in Robotics ☁️
- **IoT Basics**: MQTT, HTTP, and cloud platforms.
- **Projects**: Internet-controlled robots and cloud-based data logging.

## 11. PCB Design & Fabrication 🛠️
- **Design Tools**: Schematic creation, multi-layer PCBs.
- **Implementation**: Soldering, assembly, and testing for robotic systems.

## 12. Final Project & Industry Practices 🏆
- **Capstone Project**: Warehouse bots, AI surveillance systems, or pick-and-place arms.
- **Industry Readiness**: Case studies, resume building, and mock interviews.
